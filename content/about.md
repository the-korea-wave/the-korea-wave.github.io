[한국어 안내](#ko)

**The Korea Wave** covers Korean entertainment — K-pop, K-drama, film, and webtoons — as a global business, for readers who follow the industry and strategy behind Korean culture's international growth, not just its releases.

## What we cover

We analyze agency performance and strategy, streaming and fandom-platform economics, content export deals, and IP licensing — the business decisions that shape how Korean entertainment reaches the world.

## How our articles are made

For data stories, we calculate measures such as the share of musicals in the KOPIS weekly top 50 from the underlying records. Other pieces bring together figures and reporting from several sources. Each article links to its sources.

We use AI to help draft the text. Articles can publish automatically after source-data checks, quality checks and an AI review. Flagged articles are held for further review. HW has editorial responsibility and we spot-check published work. Read our [editorial standards](../editorial-standards/).
