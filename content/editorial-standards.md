This page sets out how our articles are produced, who is accountable for them, and what we do when we get something wrong.

## Who is responsible

**HW** is the editor of The Korea Wave and is accountable for everything published here, including corrections. HW writes under a pen name; correspondence reaches a person, not a form, at the address on our [Contact](../contact/) page.

## Where our information comes from

We work from **primary Korean-language sources**: official box-office data from KOBIS (Korean Film Council), performing-arts ticketing data from KOPIS (Korea Performing Arts Box Office Information System), company disclosures on DART where a listed entertainment company is involved, and Korean industry press reporting.

Every article lists its sources at the foot of the page. If a claim rests on a single source, we say so rather than presenting it as settled.

## What we do and do not do

- We **do not translate or republish** any single source article. Every piece is written from several sources at once, and the analysis — what the facts mean together — is ours.
- We **distinguish record from interpretation**. Where a reading of the facts is our inference rather than something a source establishes, the text says so explicitly.
- We **do not report figures we cannot trace** to a named source or filing.
- We **do not accept payment** for coverage, and we do not publish sponsored posts.

## How AI is used

We use AI-assisted drafting tools as part of production. This is disclosed rather than hidden, and it is bounded:

- **A human editor reviews and approves every article before it is published.** Nothing publishes automatically.
- Approval is a recorded step, not a formality — each article is reviewed as a specific change that is either accepted or rejected.
- Drafting assistance does not extend to sourcing: the source list for each article is derived from the filings and releases the piece is built on.

## Corrections

If an article contains an error, tell us at the address on our [Contact](../contact/) page and we will look at it.

- **Factual errors** are corrected in place, and the correction is noted on the article.
- **Errors that change the meaning** of a piece are corrected with an explicit note at the foot of the article stating what was wrong and when it was fixed.
- We do not quietly delete articles to make errors disappear. If a piece cannot be corrected, it is withdrawn with a note explaining why.

## Independence

The Korea Wave is independent and self-funded. It is not owned by, and does not act on behalf of, any company or institution it covers.
