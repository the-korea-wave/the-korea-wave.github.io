Musicals took 16 of the 50 places in Korea’s performance box office ranking for week 40 of 2026, raising their share from 24% to 32%. Pop concerts still held the largest share, with 30 entries, but musicals claimed five of the top 10 spots.

## Musicals move up the chart

*Chicago* ranked second, behind a Seoul concert by TOMORROW X TOGETHER. *Frozen*, *Elisabeth*, *Gwanghwamun Yeonga* and *Dracula* filled places four through seven. Pop concerts held four top 10 places, while a Philadelphia Orchestra performance took ninth.

## A changing weekly lineup

The top 50 included 28 new entries. Eight productions rose in rank and 14 fell. The leading genre’s share declined from 68% to 60%, even as pop concerts remained the largest group. The figures describe places in the ranking; they do not show how ticket revenue or attendance was divided among genres.
