South Korea’s box-office revenue fell 5.4% in the 44th week of 2026. Three films entered the weekly top 10, but the data does not establish what caused the overall decline.

## Chiikawa holds the lead

*극장판 치이카와: 인어 섬의 비밀* (*Chiikawa: The Secret of Mermaid Island*) ranked first with ₩3.42 billion in weekly revenue and 335,812 admissions. New entrant *하트 오브 비스트* (*Heart of Beast*) placed second with ₩2.12 billion and 216,061 admissions. *암살자(들)* (*Assassin(s)*) followed in third with ₩709.6 million.

## New arrivals reshape the top 10

The other new entries were *수능, 출제의 비밀* in fourth place, with ₩646.2 million, and *진격의 거인: 홍련의 화살* in sixth, with ₩529.1 million. Six films fell in the rankings, while none rose.

The top three films accounted for 54.4% of weekly revenue, down from 56.5% in the previous week. That narrower share shows revenue was spread more widely among the ranked films, even as the box office contracted overall.
