Musicals accounted for 38% of Korea’s 50 ranked performances in week 42 of 2026, up from 30% in the previous ranking. Popular music remained the largest category with 25 entries, or 50%, but its share fell from 56%.

## Musicals Take the Top Spot

*Sweeney Todd* ranked first, while *Elisabeth* placed ninth. In all, 19 musicals appeared in the top 50. The ranking also recorded 29 new entries, including *Sweeney Todd*, a Lee Chan-won concert in Gyeonggi and Roy Kim’s Seoul tour stop.

## Concerts Remain Prominent in the Top 10

Popular music held seven of the top 10 places. The Lee Chan-won concert ranked second, followed by Roy Kim in third. AKMU concerts in Busan and Daegu took fourth and fifth. *Pokémon The Orchestra* was the highest-ranked classical performance, at No. 10.

Across the full top 50, classical music had three entries, theater two and dance one. Five productions rose in the ranking, while 15 fell.
