“The Odyssey” remained South Korea’s top weekend movie, extending a remarkably durable theatrical run even as new titles arrived. Its continued hold on first place points to strong repeat interest and broad audience appeal rather than a brief opening-week surge.

## A theatrical run with unusual endurance

The film has led the daily box office for weeks and repeatedly defended its weekend crown. During that period, its cumulative admissions passed a major blockbuster milestone and continued to rise, placing it among the market’s biggest recent theatrical successes.

That trajectory is notable in a release environment where films often surrender the lead as soon as fresh competition reaches cinemas. “The Odyssey” instead carried its momentum through successive weekends, turning an initially strong debut into a sustained market performance.

## New releases have yet to break the hold

Competition intensified as titles including “Obsession” and “Spider-Man” drew moviegoers, but neither immediately displaced “The Odyssey” from the overall lead. The expanding slate appears to have increased traffic at theaters without sharply weakening the incumbent.

The key question is now how long the film can preserve that advantage. Its pace will eventually slow, but an extended spell at the top gives it more time to build admissions and leaves rival distributors competing for the remaining audience rather than commanding the market outright.
