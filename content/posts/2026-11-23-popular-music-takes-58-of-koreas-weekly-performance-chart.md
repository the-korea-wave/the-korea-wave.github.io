Popular music expanded its share of Korea’s top 50 box-office ranking to 58% in week 47 of 2026, up from 46% the previous week. Concerts accounted for 29 ranked productions, a gain of 12 percentage points in the genre’s share of the chart.

## Concerts lead the top ten

DAY6 Special Concert: The Present ranked first, followed by CRUSH CONCERT: CRUSH H♥UR. Popular music took six of the top ten places, including Kim Yong-bin’s Daegu tour stop at No. 4 and Forestella concerts in Ilsan and Daejeon at Nos. 5 and 10.

Musicals held the other four top-ten places. The Phantom of the Opera 40th Anniversary Special Season ranked third, while Mrs. Doubtfire, Frozen and Chicago placed sixth through eighth.

## A chart with substantial turnover

There were 32 new entries among the 50 ranked productions. Ten productions moved up and seven moved down.

Beyond the 29 popular music entries, the chart contained 17 musicals, two plays, one dance production and one circus or magic production. The 58% figure describes popular music’s share of ranked productions; it is not a share of ticket revenue.
