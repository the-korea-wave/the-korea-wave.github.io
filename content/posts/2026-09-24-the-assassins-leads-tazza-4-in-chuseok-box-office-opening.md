Yoo Hai-jin’s *The Assassin(s)* won the opening day contest against Byun Yo-han’s *Tazza: The Song of Beelzebub*. Both opened in South Korea on September 23, but the historical thriller took first place with 134,464 admissions; the fourth *Tazza* film followed with 103,362, according to the [Korean Film Council’s daily box office](https://koreanfilm.or.kr/eng/news/boxOffice_Daily.jsp?mode=BOXOFFICE_DAILY).

## An early lead, with a screen advantage

*The Assassin(s)* played on 1,415 screens, compared with 1,073 for *Tazza*. That wider opening gave the thriller more opportunities to sell tickets, though the first day’s figures alone cannot show how much screen availability contributed to the gap. Christopher Nolan’s *The Odyssey* remained in third place with 41,206 admissions.

## Two different bets on holiday audiences

Directed by Hur Jin-ho, *The Assassin(s)* follows an investigation into the 1974 shooting of South Korea’s first lady. The new *Tazza* film brings its gambling story into an online casino business and is rated for adults, while *The Assassin(s)* is rated for viewers aged 12 and over. Those differences may shape their audiences over Chuseok, but opening day establishes only the starting order. The holiday run will show whether *Tazza* can close the gap or whether *The Assassin(s)* can hold its lead.
