Ha Jung-woo has been confirmed to headline a newly announced prequel to the acclaimed 2015 political crime thriller *Inside Men*. He will be joined by a formidable ensemble cast featuring Ahn Bo-hyun, Park Hae-joon, and Jung Sung-il as production gears up to expand the celebrated cinematic franchise.

## Exploring the Roots of Corruption
The original *Inside Men* left a significant mark on Korean cinema with its gritty, unyielding portrayal of illicit ties intertwining politics, conservative media, and corporate conglomerates. By greenlighting a prequel, the upcoming production is positioned to explore the origins of those intricate alliances and the early power struggles that defined the original narrative. Ha Jung-woo assumes the central lead position, bringing his extensive background in high-stakes dramatic roles to anchor the new timeline. 

## Assembling the New Roster
The supporting cast rounds out a group of highly recognized industry heavyweights. Ahn Bo-hyun, Park Hae-joon, and Jung Sung-il are all confirmed to join the project. Each actor has recently built a strong portfolio across dark television dramas and action thrillers, setting the stage for intense on-screen dynamics. The assembly of this specific ensemble points toward a narrative heavily focused on the formative rivalries, betrayals, and backroom dealings that ultimately set the stage for the events of the 2015 blockbuster.
