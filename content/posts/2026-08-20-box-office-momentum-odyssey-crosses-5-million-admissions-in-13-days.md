The South Korean box office is experiencing a significant surge as the highly anticipated film "Odyssey" accelerates past major milestones, reaching 5 million admissions in exactly 13 days. This rapid accumulation of ticket sales highlights a revitalized theatrical landscape, with multiple major releases drawing massive crowds and setting new speed records for audience turnout. 

## Unprecedented Speed to Five Million
"Odyssey" has maintained relentless momentum since its premiere. The feature required only a single day to bridge the gap from 4 million to 5 million viewers, solidifying its position at the top of the charts. This 13-day trajectory to the 5 million mark distinctly outpaces the ticket sales velocity of several earlier domestic hits. Even in its initial days, the film showcased extraordinary draw, hitting 1.37 million admissions at a rate that easily eclipsed the early performance of "Oppenheimer." 

## Competing Blockbusters Fuel the Market
The explosive performance of "Odyssey" is complemented by the formidable debut of "Spider-Man 4," which has injected further vitality into the cinema sector. The superhero sequel rapidly mobilized audiences, securing 1.13 million ticket sales within its first two days. This opening pace surpasses the benchmark previously set by the film "Hope," pointing to a fiercely competitive season where major franchises and high-profile releases are successfully translating public anticipation into concrete box office dominance.
