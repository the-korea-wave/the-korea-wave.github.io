South Korea’s weekly box office revenue fell 8.8% in 2026-W46, while the three highest-grossing films accounted for a smaller share of sales. Their combined share dropped from 51.4% to 42.1%, a decline of 9.3 percentage points, according to KOBIS data.

## Chiikawa holds first place

*Chiikawa: The Secret of Mermaid Island* led the weekly chart with ₩1.60 billion in revenue and 160,258 admissions. Its cumulative audience reached 2,949,095.

*Fall 2: Deadpoint* entered at No. 2 with ₩1.38 billion and 138,113 admissions. *Final Interview* followed with ₩1.17 billion and 116,495 admissions.

## Revenue spreads beyond the top three

Four films entered the weekly ranking, including *Fall 2: Deadpoint*, *La La Land* and *Dune*. *La La Land* placed sixth with ₩615.5 million in revenue, while *Dune* placed seventh with ₩513.2 million.

The lower share held by the top three shows that weekly sales were spread more widely across the chart, even as overall box office revenue declined.
