South Korea’s box office revenue fell 5.4% in week 44 of 2026. Three films entered the top 10, but the leading three titles accounted for 54.5% of revenue, down from 56.5% the previous week.

## Chiikawa Holds the Top Spot

*극장판 치이카와: 인어 섬의 비밀* ranked first with ₩3.42 billion in revenue and 335,803 admissions. Its cumulative admissions reached 2.58 million.

New top-10 entry *하트 오브 비스트* took second place with ₩2.12 billion and 216,291 admissions. *암살자(들)* followed in third with ₩709.6 million and 73,621 admissions.

## Three Entries Change the Top 10

The other new entries were *수능, 출제의 비밀*, fourth with ₩646.2 million, and *진격의 거인: 홍련의 화살*, sixth with ₩529.1 million. No film rose in rank, while six fell.

The smaller revenue share for the top three shows that sales were spread more widely across the chart, even as the market’s weekly total declined.
