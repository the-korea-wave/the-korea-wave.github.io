Revenue of the top 10 films at South Korea’s box office fell 5.4% in week 44 of 2026 (Oct. 26–Nov. 1) from the previous week. Three films entered the top 10, but the leading three titles accounted for 54.5% of weekly revenue, down from 56.5% the previous week.

## Chiikawa Holds the Top Spot

*Chiikawa the Movie: The Secret of the Mermaid Island (극장판 치이카와: 인어 섬의 비밀)* ranked first with ₩3.42 billion in revenue and 335,803 admissions. Its cumulative admissions reached 2.58 million.

New top-10 entry *Heart of the Beast (하트 오브 비스트)*, which opened on Oct. 28, took second place with ₩2.12 billion and 216,291 admissions. *The Assassin(s) (암살자(들))* followed in third with ₩709.6 million and 73,621 admissions.

## Three Entries Change the Top 10

The other new entries were *Ugly Duckling Ms. Maeng (수능, 출제의 비밀)*, also released on Oct. 28, fourth with ₩646.2 million, and *Attack on Titan: Crimson Arrows (진격의 거인: 홍련의 화살)*, a 2015 release, sixth with ₩529.1 million. No film rose in rank, while six fell.

The smaller revenue share for the top three shows that sales were spread more widely across the chart, even as the top 10’s weekly total declined.
