Revenue from South Korea’s top 10 films fell 45.9% in week 42 of 2026 (Oct. 12–18). *Chiikawa the Movie: The Secret of the Mermaid Island (극장판 치이카와: 인어 섬의 비밀)* led the chart, while three films released on Oct. 14 entered the top 10. The top three films accounted for 66.5% of revenue, down from 70.7% the previous week.

## Chiikawa holds a wide lead

*Chiikawa the Movie: The Secret of the Mermaid Island (극장판 치이카와: 인어 섬의 비밀)* earned ₩5.53 billion from 547,462 admissions. Second-place *The Assassin(s) (암살자(들))* took ₩2.48 billion from 255,330 admissions, followed by *Tazza: The Song of Beelzebub (타짜: 벨제붑의 노래)* with ₩1.68 billion from 166,468 admissions.

The leader earned more than twice as much as the second-place film. Even so, the top three’s share of revenue fell by 4.2 percentage points, leaving a larger share for films further down the chart.

## New arrivals enter below the top four

The three films that entered the top 10 this week placed fifth, seventh and eighth. *Whalefall (웨일폴: 고래에 먹힌 남자)* earned ₩566.7 million, *Guardians of the Video Game (전자오락수호대)* earned ₩359.5 million, and *Street Fighter (스트리트 파이터)* earned ₩321.1 million.

*The Odyssey (오디세이)* remained ahead of those arrivals in fourth place with ₩1.30 billion. Its cumulative admissions reached 12.27 million, the highest total among this week’s top 10.
