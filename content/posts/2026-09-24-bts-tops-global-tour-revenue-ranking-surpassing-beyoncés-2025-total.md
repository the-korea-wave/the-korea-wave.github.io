BTS’s *ARIRANG* world tour leads Pollstar’s global touring ranking through the third quarter of 2026. Its reported $465.8 million in ticket revenue also exceeds the $407.6 million that Beyoncé’s *Cowboy Carter Tour* earned across Pollstar’s 2025 ranking. The comparison is between BTS’s current tally and Beyoncé’s prior-year total, rather than two tours competing in the same period. [Pollstar’s third-quarter analysis](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/09/22/strength-in-numbers-a-record-setting-q3-surpasses-6b-bts-bad-bunny-the-weeknd-top-charts-q3-analysis/)

## Stadium crowds drove the lead

Pollstar counted 2,314,140 tickets sold across 38 BTS shows. Four concerts at Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas alone drew 245,691 attendees and grossed $66.6 million. Bad Bunny placed second in the current ranking with $438.6 million, leaving BTS ahead by $27.2 million. [Pollstar’s third-quarter analysis](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/09/22/strength-in-numbers-a-record-setting-q3-surpasses-6b-bts-bad-bunny-the-weeknd-top-charts-q3-analysis/)

## A strong year for the largest tours

The ranking reflects reported ticket sales within Pollstar’s third-quarter reporting window, which ran from November 13, 2025, through August 12, 2026. Across the top 100 touring artists, revenue reached $6.12 billion. BTS’s lead therefore comes amid a record third-quarter total for the industry’s biggest tours. The *ARIRANG* tour remains underway, so its final gross has yet to be determined.
