Musicals expanded their share of Korea’s weekly top 50 performances to 38% in week 45 of 2026, up from 36% the previous week. They accounted for 19 ranked productions, while popular music remained the largest category with 25.

## Musicals claim half of the top 10

Five musicals placed in the top 10. The Phantom of the Opera 40th Anniversary Special Season ranked second, followed by Sweeney Todd in third. Chicago, Frozen and Rebecca also made the top 10.

NCT WISH’s *Face Our WISH* concert in Seoul led the ranking. N.Flying’s *Once Upon a Snowball* placed fourth, giving popular music two of the top four positions.

## A busy week for new entries

The ranking included 33 new entries, among them the NCT WISH concert, Sweeney Todd and the N.Flying show. Eight productions rose in position and nine fell.

Popular music’s share of the top 50 declined from 56% to 50% as musicals gained two percentage points. These figures describe the mix of ranked performances, rather than ticket sales or audience size.
