Popular dance took 8% of Korea’s weekly top 50 performances in 2026-W41, up from 0% the previous week. Its four entries made the ranking more varied, though popular music remained the largest genre with 28 entries.

## A broader mix in the top 50

Musicals accounted for 15 ranked performances, followed by popular dance with four, theater with two and Western classical music with one. The leading genre’s share fell from 62% to 56%, reflecting a less concentrated ranking.

The list also changed substantially: 32 productions were new entries, while three climbed and 15 fell. Among the newcomers were *The Phantom of the Opera 40th Anniversary Special Season*, *DAVICHI CONCERT: IN OUR SCENES* and *JEONGHAN X JOSHUA JOURNEY INTO: DREAMING* in Incheon.

## Concerts still shape the top ten

*The Phantom of the Opera 40th Anniversary Special Season* ranked first, followed by Davichi’s concert and Epik High’s concert. Popular music occupied eight of the top ten places; musicals held the other two. The top ten included six Seoul performances, two in Incheon, and one each in Busan and Daejeon.

Popular dance’s arrival is a shift in the **composition of the ranking**. The figures show how many productions placed in the top 50, rather than their ticket sales or revenue.
