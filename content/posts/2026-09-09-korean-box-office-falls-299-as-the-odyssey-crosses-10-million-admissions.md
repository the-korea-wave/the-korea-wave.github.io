Korea's theatrical market cooled sharply in the week of August 31 through September 6, with total weekly ticket revenue falling 29.9% from the prior week — even as *The Odyssey* pushed past the 10 million cumulative admissions mark, the country's traditional benchmark for a blockbuster. Four films entered the top 10, but none of them came close to offsetting the pullback at the top of the chart.

## A 10-Million Milestone at the Top

*The Odyssey* held first place with ₩13.43 billion in weekly revenue from 1,163,094 admissions, lifting its cumulative total to 10,027,961 tickets sold. That weekly haul was roughly 6.2 times the take of second-place *Spider-Man: Brand New Day*, which earned ₩2.17 billion from 243,640 admissions and now stands at 8,792,022 cumulative admissions — still short of the 10 million line its rival just crossed.

The gap between first and second place, in other words, remains the defining feature of the chart. Even in a down week, the market's decline was largely a story of its biggest title selling fewer tickets, not of audiences migrating elsewhere.

## Four Debuts, Modest Landings

The week's four new entries — *Obsession* at third, *Bigwang* at fifth, *Sing Again* at sixth, and *The Intern* at tenth — combined for about ₩3.28 billion, less than a quarter of what *The Odyssey* earned on its own. *Obsession* led the newcomers with ₩2.0 billion from 184,316 admissions, while *Bigwang* (₩635 million) and *Sing Again* (₩524 million) opened in the mid-table. *The Intern* entered with ₩122 million from 11,989 admissions.

The rest of the chart moved in one direction: no film climbed in rank, and four fell. Holdovers *Gyeongju Travelogue* (₩998 million, fourth) and *The Last Day on Oak Street* (₩225 million, seventh) rounded out the live-action field, with family and animation titles *Heartsping: Legend of the Whale Jewel* (cumulative 858,707 admissions) and *Detective Conan: The Fallen Angel of the Highway* (cumulative 420,629) holding eighth and ninth.

## Concentration Eases, but Only Slightly

The share of weekly revenue captured by the top three films slipped from 84.9% to 80.6%. The four debuts spread the take marginally wider, yet four of every five won spent at the Korean box office still went to just three titles. Until a challenger opens at genuine scale, the market's weekly fortunes will continue to rise and fall with a single film's screen count and staying power.
