Combined sales of South Korea’s top 10 films rose 43.0% from the previous week in week 47 of 2026 (Nov. 16–22), led by the new release *White Blast* (폭설), which opened on Nov. 18. The film drew 419,355 viewers and earned ₩4.19 billion during the week, according to KOBIS data.

## New arrivals reshape the chart

Three films entered the weekly top 10, all of them released on Nov. 18: *White Blast*, *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* (헝거게임 50번째 추첨의 날) and *LIM YOUNG WOONG | IM HERO THE STADIUM 2* (임영웅ㅣ아임 히어로 더 스타디움 2). The latter drew 61,861 viewers but earned ₩1.71 billion, more revenue than any other top-10 film except *White Blast*.

*Chiikawa the Movie: The Secret of the Mermaid Island* (극장판 치이카와: 인어 섬의 비밀) placed second with 120,333 viewers and ₩1.21 billion in weekly revenue. *Fall 2: Deadpoint* (폴2: 데드포인트) followed with 100,798 viewers and ₩994 million.

## Revenue becomes more concentrated

The top three films in the attendance ranking took 50.3% of total weekly box office sales, up from 42.1% a week earlier, an increase of 8.2 percentage points. Of the seven films that were also in the previous week’s top 10, six fell in the rankings and *LA LA LAND* (라라랜드) held its place.
