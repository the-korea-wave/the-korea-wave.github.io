South Korea’s weekly box office revenue rose 43.0% in week 47 of 2026, led by the new release *폭설*. The film drew 419,355 viewers and earned ₩4.19 billion during the week, according to KOBIS data.

## New arrivals reshape the chart

Three films entered the weekly top 10: *폭설*, *헝거게임 50번째 추첨의 날* and *임영웅ㅣ아임 히어로 더 스타디움 2*. The latter drew 61,861 viewers but earned ₩1.71 billion, more revenue than any listed film except *폭설*.

*극장판 치이카와: 인어 섬의 비밀* placed second with 120,333 viewers and ₩1.21 billion in weekly revenue. *폴2: 데드포인트* followed with 100,798 viewers and ₩994 million.

## Revenue becomes more concentrated

The top three films’ share of weekly revenue climbed from 42.1% to 50.3%, an increase of 8.2 percentage points. Six films fell in the rankings, while none moved up.
