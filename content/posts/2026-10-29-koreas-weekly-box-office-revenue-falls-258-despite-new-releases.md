Revenue from South Korea’s top 10 films fell 25.8% in week 43 of 2026 (Oct. 19–25). Five films released on Oct. 21–22 entered the top 10, but the new arrivals did not offset the drop in overall sales.

## Chiikawa holds the lead

*Chiikawa the Movie: The Secret of the Mermaid Island (극장판 치이카와: 인어 섬의 비밀)* ranked first with ₩4.31 billion in revenue and 419,214 admissions. Its cumulative admissions reached 2,248,043. *The Assassin(s) (암살자(들))* followed with ₩1.23 billion and 125,350 admissions, while new entrant *Paradise Lost (실낙원)* took third with ₩997.94 million and 102,780 admissions.

## Revenue spreads beyond the top three

The top three films accounted for 56.5% of weekly revenue, down from 66.4% the previous week, a decline of 9.9 percentage points. Other new entries included *Clayface (클레이페이스)* in fifth place with ₩788.86 million and *Still Shining (여전히 찬란하게)* in seventh with ₩330.38 million.

The narrower concentration shows that sales were spread more widely across the chart. It did not mean a stronger market overall: top 10 revenue still declined by more than a quarter.
