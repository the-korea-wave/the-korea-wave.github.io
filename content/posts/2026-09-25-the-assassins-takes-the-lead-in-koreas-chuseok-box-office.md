*The Assassin(s)* has turned its advance-ticket momentum into a clear lead at South Korea’s Chuseok box office. It drew 288,489 viewers on September 24, more than twice the 143,994 recorded by *Tazza: The Song of Beelzebub*, commonly called *Tazza 4*. Together, the two films accounted for nearly 60% of the day’s box office revenue, according to [Korean Film Council data](https://m.koreanfilm.or.kr/eng/news/boxOffice_Daily.jsp?mode=BOXOFFICE_DAILY).

## The lead widened after opening day

Both films opened on September 23. *The Assassin(s)* sold 134,462 tickets that day, compared with 103,366 for *Tazza 4*. On their second day, attendance rose for both, but *The Assassin(s)* pulled further ahead. By the end of September 24, their cumulative admissions stood at 447,077 and 266,543, respectively.

## Two films are drawing the holiday crowd

The figures support the early expectation that *The Assassin(s)* would be a Chuseok draw. They also show why this is more than a single-film story: *Tazza 4* remained firmly in second place as holiday attendance increased. The first two days establish a strong opening for both films, with *The Assassin(s)* holding the larger share; they do not yet settle how the full holiday run will finish.
