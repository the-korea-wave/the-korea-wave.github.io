Musicals accounted for 23 of the 50 productions in Korea’s weekly performance box office ranking, lifting their share from 36% to 46%. The 10 percentage point gain brought musicals nearly level with popular music, which had 22 entries.

## New arrivals reshape the ranking

Twenty-eight productions entered the top 50 this week. Among them were the Busan run of *Hamilton*, which debuted at No. 2, HYUKOH’s Seoul tour stop at No. 3, and TOUCHED’s *HIGHLIGHT Ⅴ* concert at No. 5. Six productions rose in the ranking, while 15 fell.

The remaining top 50 entries comprised three plays, one dance production and one circus or magic production. KOPIS’s measure of concentration in the leading genre declined from 52% to 46%, despite musicals’ growing presence.

## Concerts retain the top spot

Sung Si-kyung’s year-end concert ranked No. 1. Popular music productions held six of the top 10 places; musicals held four, including *Chicago* at No. 7, *A Tale of Two Cities* at No. 8 and *The Phantom of the Opera* 40th anniversary special season at No. 9.
