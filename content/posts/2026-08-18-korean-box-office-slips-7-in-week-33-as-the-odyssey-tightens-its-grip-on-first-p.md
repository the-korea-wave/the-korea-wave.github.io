South Korea's box office cooled in the 33rd week of 2026, with total weekly ticket revenue falling 7.0% from the prior week even as *The Odyssey* held a commanding lead at the top of the chart. The decline came alongside a modest loosening of the market's extreme top-heaviness: the top three films' share of weekly revenue eased to 88.5% from 91.1% a week earlier, according to data from the Korean Film Council's integrated ticketing system (KOBIS).

## A Two-Film Race at the Top

*The Odyssey* remained the week's dominant title by a wide margin, grossing ₩29.41 billion from 2,667,695 admissions and pushing its cumulative audience to 4,542,453. That weekly haul was more than double that of second-place *Spider-Man: Brand New Day*, which took in ₩14.0 billion from 1,363,012 admissions.

The cumulative figures tell a different story about the two films' trajectories. *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* has now drawn 7,279,368 total admissions in Korea — well ahead of *The Odyssey*'s running total — but its weekly audience is now roughly half the newer release's, suggesting the superhero title is deep into the back end of its run while *The Odyssey* is still absorbing the bulk of current demand.

Third place went to *Detective Conan: The Fallen Angel of the Highway*, the latest entry in the long-running Japanese animation franchise, which earned ₩2.13 billion from 217,199 admissions in what appears to be an early stage of its release, with cumulative admissions at 226,027.

## Two Newcomers Enter Mid-Chart

The week brought two fresh entries to the top ten. The Korean comedy sequel *Okay Madam 2* debuted in fifth place with ₩1.78 billion in revenue and 192,184 admissions, landing just behind the children's animation *Heartsping: The Legend of the Whale Jewel*, which held fourth with ₩1.87 billion. Further down, *PAW Patrol: The Dino Movie* opened in seventh with ₩424.7 million from 43,879 admissions.

Beyond the new arrivals, movement across the chart skewed negative: two titles climbed in the rankings while five fell, consistent with a week in which overall spending contracted.

## Long Runners and the Lower Half

*Hope* continued its extended run in sixth place, adding 81,690 admissions for a cumulative audience of 4,475,556 — a total that now rivals *The Odyssey*'s, accumulated over a far longer window. The bottom of the chart was rounded out by *What Should We Have Done?* in eighth (₩194.2 million), *Minions & Monsters* in ninth with a cumulative audience of 772,750, and *David* in tenth, which has drawn 298,694 admissions to date.

## Reading the Concentration Numbers

The drop in top-three revenue concentration from 91.1% to 88.5% is small in absolute terms, but it marks a slight redistribution of spending toward the middle of the chart — driven largely by *Okay Madam 2*'s debut and the steady family-audience business of *Heartsping*. Even so, a market where fewer than nine of every ten won still flow to just three titles remains unusually concentrated, and the 7.0% weekly revenue decline indicates the mid-August holdovers are not yet generating enough breadth to offset the gradual cooldown of the summer's biggest releases.
